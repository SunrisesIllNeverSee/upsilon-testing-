## Publication boundary

The financial paper can publish:
- the three-state formalism;
- commitment-lineage schema at the research abstraction level;
- EDGAR corpus construction;
- annotation protocol;
- amendment-instruction taxonomy;
- reconstruction metrics;
- held-out results;
- reproducibility harness sufficient to validate reported results.

Do not automatically publish:
- proprietary enforcement/gating logic;
- internal recovery/collapse mechanisms;
- production policy logic;
- proprietary scoring/calibration;
- non-public downstream customer data;
- deployment secrets.

## Repository policy

This MVP repository should be treated as private until the publication/IP boundary
is deliberately finalized. Do not attach an open-source license by default.
